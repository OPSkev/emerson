What it means

Custom is the better lane

20.4% vs 12.5% — custom out-margins brokered by ~8 points. Every point of mix shifted toward custom lifts the blended margin.

We leak between quote and reality

Both lines keep far less than they're priced for. Custom bleeds ~12 pts; brokered ~18–27. The gap — not the price — is where the margin goes.

The brokered gap is the bigger one

Resale should be clean margin, yet it keeps the least. Freight, handling, receiving and damage are eating it. Worth a hard look before we lean on brokered to scale.

The strategic fork: lean on brokered and $4.725M is reachable with little plant investment — but it earns the least and builds nothing. Lean on custom and it earns ~$373K more, but only if we build the finishing capacity to make it. The target and the capacity plan are the same decision.

One assumption to confirm before this goes wider: these margins appear to be full-cost (production labor included, plus freight/handling on brokered) — which is why they land well below a materials-only view. If that's right, the erosion on custom is essentially the shop's rework and overruns showing up on the P&L. Bryan — that's the number I most want you to poke at.

Revenue grows steadily — +35% then +11%. The step that breaks the trend is 2027 → 2028: +88%, nearly doubling in two years. That's not a sales-as-usual number — it's the plant-capacity decision.
*Gross profit at today's ~71/29 mix (18.1% blended). Change the mix on the dial above and the target's gross profit moves ~$186K.

What it means

Custom is the better lane

20.4% vs 12.5% — custom out-margins brokered by ~8 points. Every point of mix toward custom lifts the blended margin.

The 88% jump is capacity, not sales

Steady +11–35% growth, then a near-double to hit $4.725M. Reaching it on custom means roughly 2–3× today's shop output.

Mix and capacity are one decision

Lean brokered → reachable with little plant investment, but ~$777K GP and builds nothing. Lean custom → ~$964K GP, but only if we build finishing capacity.

One assumption to confirm before this goes wider: these margins look like full-cost (production labor included, plus freight/handling on brokered) — which is why they sit well below a materials-only view. If that's right, the custom erosion is essentially the shop's rework and overruns landing on the P&L. Bryan — that's the number to poke at.

How to read this

This is everything the cabinet shop charged to supplies, repairs and the van from January through July. It does not include wood, doors, hinges that go on a job, or anything billed to a customer. Those are job costs and get tracked separately.

Tap biggest buys on any row to see the two largest orders in that group.

The point is not to stop buying what we need. It is so everyone can see where the money goes and speak up when there is a cheaper way to do the same job.

Source: company spending reports, cabinet shop (CAB) class only. Updated September 2026. Questions go to Kevin Snyder.

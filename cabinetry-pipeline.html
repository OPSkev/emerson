Lead Stage
Step 1 New ▼

What it is

  • Someone reaches out — GC, homeowner, designer, or repeat client
  • Lead is entered in the system but no contact has been made yet
  • Could come through website, phone, trade show, or rep relationship

Qualifies to move

  • Sales rep reaches out to make initial contact
▼
Step 2 Contacted ▼

What it is

  • Rep has made contact with the client or GC
  • Gathering project info: scope, budget, and timeline
  • Initial call or meeting scheduled; scope of work identified
  • Budget range discussed and timeline confirmed

Qualifies to move

  • Enough info gathered to price the job
  • Rep submits budget pricing to the client
▼
Step 3 Cabinetry — Submitted ▼

What it is

  • Budget pricing has been sent to the client — waiting on their response
  • Ops builds the initial budget estimate
  • Rep delivers pricing to client/GC and manages follow-up questions

Qualifies to move

  • Client confirms they want to move forward
  • Rep determines it is a real job worth pursuing

Lost / Exits here when

  • Client goes quiet or budget doesn't fit
  • Project is not viable to pursue — mark lead Lost with reason noted
▼
Step 4 Qualified ▼

What it is

  • Lead is confirmed as a real opportunity worth pursuing
  • This is the trigger to create Scopes and an Opportunity record in the CRM
  • The job gets a number here — moves into the Sales pipeline

Qualifies to move

  • Scopes and Opportunity are created in the CRM
  • Job number is assigned
  • Moves into the Sales pipeline
Sales / CRM
Entry Opportunity Created
Overall Status: Opportunity
▼

What it is

  • A formal record exists in the CRM — Opportunity linked to a client, company, and Cabinetry scope
  • Scope is created with an Estimated Value and set to "Open" status
  • Job number is assigned at this step
  • Overall Status = "Opportunity" — no scopes won yet

Qualifies to move

  • Enough information to begin estimating — scope description, drawings, or specs available
  • Sent to ops to queue for estimating
  • Ops Quote Status set to "Queued for Estimating"
▼
Stage 1 Bid
Workflow Type: Opportunity
▼

What it is

  • Operations reviews the scope and builds a quote — material costs, labor, lead times, margins
  • Ops Quote Status flows: Queued for Estimating → In Estimating → Ready for Rep
  • May pause at "Waiting on Customer Info" (missing specs) or "Waiting on Internal Input"
  • Proposal Due Date is tracked — late quotes cost jobs
  • Award Status = "Quoting"

Qualifies to move

  • Quote is complete and reviewed by ops
  • Ops Quote Status = "Ready for Rep"
  • Sales rep picks it up and prepares to deliver the proposal

Lost / Exits here when

  • Client cancels or goes quiet before a proposal is even sent
  • Project is not viable to quote (too small, budget mismatch, out of scope)
  • Set Scope Status → Lost or On Hold
▼
Sales Proposal Sent
Award Status: Quoting
▼

What it is

  • Sales rep delivers the formal proposal to the GC or client
  • Proposal Date is logged on the Scope record
  • Rep manages follow-up, handles questions, negotiates if needed
  • Probability % on the scope reflects confidence of winning
  • Expected Award Date tracked for pipeline forecasting

Qualifies to move

  • Client/GC verbally or formally accepts the quote
  • Contract signed or purchase order / letter of intent received
  • Scope Status flips to "Won" → triggers Overall Status change
  • Award Status → "Awarded"

Stage 3 Job Awarded
Workflow Type: Project
▼

What it is

  • Official handoff from sales to operations
  • The job now exists as a Project — a PM is assigned, a Job record is linked
  • Scope Status → Won · Award Status → Awarded
  • Overall Status = "Project (Open Scopes)"
  • Production planning begins — delivery dates, lead times, engineering queue established
  • Placeholder Work Orders are created manually at this stage — triggered by the scope flipping to Won
  • Placeholder WOs hold the production slot and show capacity impact before engineering begins — no drawings or materials needed yet

Qualifies to move

  • PM is assigned and confirmed
  • Scope locked and client contact established
  • Delivery dates and lead times established
  • Placeholder WOs created to hold the production slot
  • Engineering queue set up — moves to Design (Stage 2)
Engineering / Pre-Production
Stage 2 Design
Cabinetry Workflow Type: Project
▼

What it is

  • Cabinet Vision drawings built out — layouts, elevations, door styles, hardware, finishes
  • Pre-production drawings handled by the PM
  • Client and/or designer must approve the design before engineering can proceed
  • Door style, wood species, finish, and hardware decisions must all be locked in here

Qualifies to move

  • Cabinet Vision drawings complete and approved by client/designer
  • All client decisions finalized — door style, hardware, finish, wood species
  • No outstanding questions that would require a redesign
  • Moves to Engineering Not Started (Stage 4) once design is locked

Gets stuck here when

  • Client hasn't made decisions (door style, hardware, finish) — major bottleneck source
  • Designer changes scope mid-design, requiring a restart
  • Client decision deadline not set or enforced → delays cascade into engineering and production
▼
Stage 4 Engineering Not Started
Cabinetry Workflow Type: Project
▼

What it is

  • Engineering is in the queue but no one has started yet
  • All inputs exist — design finalized, scope clear — but engineer hasn't been assigned
  • Sitting here too long is a bottleneck indicator — days at current stage is tracked
  • DraftsPerson and Engineer fields on the Work Order get assigned here

Qualifies to move

  • An engineer (Gabe, Mike, Brian) is assigned and begins work
  • Cabinet Vision drawings are complete and locked
  • Shop drawings / panel drawings ready to begin
▼
Stage 5 Engineering In Progress
Cabinetry Workflow Type: Project
▼

What it is

  • Active engineering — panel drawings, block sheets, cut sheets, CNC programs being built
  • Cabinet Vision output → CNC files, hardware pull lists, assembly drawings
  • Knife specs flagged if custom profiles are needed — Russell's grinding room scheduled
  • WO sent to CAD field tracks whether drawings are attached to the work order file

Qualifies to move

  • All drawings complete and attached to the WO file
  • CNC programs ready (if CNC involved)
  • Custom knife profiles ordered / confirmed with Russell if needed
  • No outstanding client decisions that affect the build
  • "Production Ready": decisions finalized, CAD complete, WOs created, materials onsite

Gets stuck here when

  • Engineering is behind schedule (e.g., Martha Retreat situation)
  • Client changes scope after engineering has started — requires partial or full redo
  • Missing information forces the engineer to pause and wait
▼
Stage 6 Brokerage Order In Progress
Hardware & Purchased Items
▼

What it is

  • Brokerage cabinet orders are being written, sent to vendor, and confirmations checked
  • Order requests for specialty items (glass, hardware, brackets, sink mounts) added to the AT Order page
  • PBO (Provided By Owner) requests are submitted through the AT Order Request page
  • Custom to Brokerage Work Order requests are submitted

Qualifies to move

  • All hardware and purchased items have been ordered (POs sent)
  • Vendor confirmations received with expected delivery dates
  • No back-ordered items that would block production or delivery
▼
Stage 7 Finalize Material / Brokerage Orders
Overall: Project (Awaiting WOs)
▼

What it is

  • Final confirmation that all materials and hardware orders are accounted for
  • Lumber, sheet goods, hardware all verified against WO requirements
  • Back Order Items? field flags anything that could hold production
  • Production Work Orders are formally created here (post-engineering) — these are separate from Placeholder WOs created at Stage 3
  • Placeholder WOs are promoted to Active once materials are confirmed and engineering is complete
  • PBO (Provided By Owner) items confirmed and tracked through the AT Order Request page
  • FEO review completed and signed off before Work Orders are released

Qualifies to move

  • All materials confirmed — in-house or confirmed ETA from vendor
  • Work Orders fully created and linked to the scope
  • WO Type = Active (not Placeholder or Pending)
  • Ready to hand off to production for final review
Production
Stage 8 Final WO Review
Cabinetry Workflow Type: Production
▼

What it is

  • Work Order gets a final quality check before it hits the shop floor
  • MOC review triggered by "Ready for MOC Review" flag
  • MOC verifies: drawings attached, CNC files flagged, hardware listed, knife profiles confirmed, paint/stain grade noted
  • Lumber source confirmed (lock shed, warehouse, etc.)
  • Sub Work Orders confirmed
  • HBIC review completed as final sign-off

Qualifies to move

  • MOC and HBIC reviews complete and signed off
  • WO is Released (Released field checked) — formal release to the shop
  • All drawings and files confirmed attached to the WO
  • Materials confirmed on-hand or arriving imminently
▼
Stage 9 Ready for Production
Cabinetry Workflow Type: Production
▼

What it is

  • WO is on the production schedule — shop has everything it needs but hasn't started yet
  • Queued in the build schedule based on due date and capacity
  • Materials are physically staged at the shop
  • WO Need By Date is firm and the schedule is built around it

Qualifies to move

  • Shop floor picks up the WO and begins actual build work
  • First daily progress entry logged (Daily Earned Revenue tracking starts)
  • CNC starts running, frames start getting cut, or assembly begins

Gets stuck here when

  • Materials didn't show up on time (vendor delay, back order)
  • Higher-priority WOs bump it in the queue (capacity conflict)
  • Knife not ground yet or CNC program not fully ready
▼
Stage 10 In Production
Cabinetry Workflow Type: Production
▼

What it is

  • Active build in the shop — CNC running, frames built, doors made, boxes assembled, finishing applied
  • Daily Earned Revenue tracked — progress updated each day
  • WO Status and Progress % visible to management in real time
  • Sub Work Orders may exist for specific components (CNC, glue-up, doors)
  • Days Behind and Off Track? fields flag schedule risk

Qualifies to move

  • Build 100% complete — all line items finished
  • Quality check passed by shop supervisor
  • Product packed, stacked, and ready for pickup or staging
  • Progress = 100% on all work order line items

Gets stuck here when

  • Incorrect drawings or programs requiring rework
  • Color samples not approved before production began
  • Material not ready — missing components holding up the build
▼
Stage 11 Staged
Workflow Type: Production
▼

What it is

  • Product fully built and physically staged in the shop or warehouse — waiting on delivery
  • May be waiting on: job site readiness, install schedule slot, or phase sequencing
  • Show on Delivery Dashboard field is checked so delivery team can see it's ready
  • For large projects, many WOs can be staged simultaneously waiting for phase sequencing

Qualifies to move

  • Delivery date confirmed with the GC or client
  • Delivery Confirmed? field is checked
  • Truck is scheduled and route confirmed
  • Site is ready to receive product

Gets stuck here when

  • Site is not ready — GC pushed the schedule, other trades not done
  • Too much staged product ties up shop floor space
  • Phase sequencing holds product (Phase 2 can't go until Phase 1 install is confirmed)
▼
Stage 12 ✓ Delivered
Overall: Ready to Complete
▼

What it is

  • Product physically on the job site — truck delivered, product offloaded
  • Delivery Ticket generated and signed by site contact
  • Delivery photos archived in Airtable
  • Billing triggered — Billing record created, invoice sent
  • When ALL Work Orders reach Delivered → Overall Status becomes "Ready to Complete"

Project is complete when

  • All WOs delivered and billing submitted
  • No outstanding Field Work Orders remaining
  • Closeout punch list items resolved
  • Final payment received → project fully closed
